## Affordable proposed sequence

1. **Free preparation:** verify founder/member facts, gather actual public releases/brand-use dates, and organize permitted title/domain evidence in a restricted inventory. Existing research has no qualifying first-use-in-commerce date or verified future-LLC title. Continue using the research packet; a `.com` purchase is optional, not a prerequisite.
2. **Pay for judgment first:** after separate authorization, seek a capped, assessment-only trademark engagement using [brief](ATTORNEY_BRIEF.md). Ask for practical registrability/use-risk advice, priority and relatedness of AI/software candidates, proper applicant/use basis, and `.com` implications. A small initial review may identify stop/go research needs; it is not necessarily a comprehensive clearance opinion. If a sufficient scope cannot fit the ceiling, obtain a narrower defined deliverable or defer for a new human budget decision. No engagement or quote request sent.
3. **Decide formation separately:** confirm owners, management, name, agent and actual need. Self-filing has a $150 state fee; an eligible consenting individual agent avoids vendor charges but brings address/process-handling responsibilities. If an external agent is wanted, compare verified first-year/renewal totals, not a $0 headline. Buy limited Illinois agreement/title advice when those facts are complex, rather than a broad subscription or tax-election upsell. Neither self-filing nor formation clears the mark.
4. **Defer trademark filing until counsel resolves scope/owner/basis:** a possible later two-class application has $700 base government fees before attorney work/surcharges/use filings. Filing is a separately funded/approved phase, outside the initial assessment/acquisition envelope. No deadline is invented from another applicant's case.
5. **Consider `.com` only after the risk assessment:** founder chooses direct authorized inquiry versus one broker, with a private walk-away cap and separate contact authority. Verify seller authority and proposed asset scope before any offer. Buy purchase-agreement/rights advice if domain goodwill, settlement or IP language is involved. If price or ancillary expenses exceed either sub-ceiling, defer; unused attorney budget is not permission to increase acquisition limits.

## Minimal Illinois self-filing checklist — preparation only

Authoritative mechanics: [SOS current fees](https://www.ilsos.gov/publications/business-services/llc.html), [LLC-5.5](https://www.ilsos.gov/content/dam/publications/pdf_publications/llc55.pdf), [guide](https://www.ilsos.gov/content/dam/publications/pdf_publications/c334.pdf), refreshed 2026-10-13. Reuse BF-01–07 for detailed requirements.

- [ ] Confirm prospective owners, management and Illinois applicability with needed qualified advice; one-founder status does not establish sole membership.
- [ ] Check state distinguishability and LLC designator; separate trademark review. Decide exact name/effective date and agent consent/Illinois office. Verify principal/management addresses and disclosure implications.
- [ ] Prepare articles offline with purpose, duration, member/manager and organizer information; prepare agreement outline and member/contribution records. Do not enter personal data in this public repo.
- [ ] **STOP FOR HUMAN:** review final articles, agreement choices, signatory authority, agent and exact $150 fee/current method. Only an independently authorized human may sign, submit and pay through [official SOS](https://apps.ilsos.gov/llcarticles/); no vendor checkout opened for submission.
- [ ] After any future approved acceptance, preserve stamped articles/receipt in restricted records; verify effective date. If needed, obtain EIN directly from [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) for $0 after formation, with separate authorization.
- [ ] Record annual report/agent renewals; determine actual IDOR/local/tax obligations with appropriate accountant/counsel. Recheck [FinCEN](https://www.fincen.gov/boi): current guidance exempts U.S.-created entities from BOI reporting; do not purchase stale blanket BOI packages.
- [ ] Keep personal/entity accounts and contracts correctly identified; review asset licenses/title separately. Formation transfers no existing software, goodwill or domains.

## `.com` acquisition feasibility — public information only

Refreshed 2026-10-13: [homepage](https://www.usekarma.com/) still advertises AI/ML sales-lead scoring/private beta; [RDAP](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/usekarma.com) lists Cloudflare and `client transfer prohibited`, a transfer-status flag, not seller willingness or absolute nontransferability. PR #4's E-C04 Legion-domain clue remains a possible association only. No seller identity, price, for-sale listing or willingness is verified. A public `"usekarma.com" "for sale"` search supplied no usable listing; direct Sedo/GoDaddy listing lookups failed. None proves it cannot be sold.

Options: retain current addresses and defer acquisition ($0 negotiation spend); direct human inquiry after approval ($0 broker commission, but time, escrow/contract costs); or a single approved buyer broker (published fees in comparison). Do not stack nonrefundable brokers. Acquisition of a domain alone would not clear USEKARMA against all parties or acquire trademarks, software, goodwill or historical rights. Obtain counsel advice on any broader rights/settlement language. Neither a trademark application nor desire for the address establishes entitlement to it; [ICANN UDRP](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/policy-2024-02-21-en) requires separate dispute criteria, and no dispute is proposed.

Model total as `price + broker upfront + success commission + separately required transfer/escrow/payment/renewal/tax/legal charges`. Deduplicate fees included in a written broker package. Public prices do not value this particular domain. The private ceiling calculation shows both standard broker models leave almost no allowance for extras at the maximum purchase assumption; direct approved negotiation or a lower price is more budget-compatible. No valuation, seller offer, purchase or broker engagement occurred.
