## Proposed organizational foundation

Prepare a counsel-reviewed operating-agreement outline covering members and contributions; member/manager authority; human approval for legal/financial actions; IP and domain schedules; licensing authority; admission/exit; disputes; dissolution; records and tax responsibilities. Repository governance does not substitute for an operating agreement. Neither GitHub permissions nor the charter appoints LLC members or managers.

Proposed agent comparison: an eligible consenting individual may reduce service cost but requires reliable process handling and consideration of address exposure; an authorized commercial agent may separate business service from a personal address but introduces recurring contractual cost. No provider was selected, priced, contacted, or retained.

If formation is later approved, the human should separately approve the exact articles, legal name, agent consent, membership/management, effective date, operating agreement, tax plan, and payment. Then establish a calendar from the actual filing date, maintain agent/office details and records, and review annual-report and tax duties. This is a proposed checklist, not execution authority.

## Uncertainty and questions for qualified Illinois counsel

- Is an ordinary Illinois LLC appropriate for the research/open-source/commercial model and intended owners? Would licensed professional activities require another structure?
- Which effective statutory versions, local requirements, foreign qualification, employment, and tax obligations apply to the actual locations and services?
- Should the legal entity use a different name while trademark clearance proceeds? Is an assumed-name filing needed for the chosen use?
- What operating-agreement provisions preserve the human authority and consent boundaries without making technical charter proposals binding unintentionally?
- What liability, insurance, banking, and capital-contribution arrangements are appropriate? No protection, exemption, tax election, or formation status is assumed here.
