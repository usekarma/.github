## P0 — questions blocking a reliable clearance assessment

1. **What do complete searches show for each of USEKARMA, USE KARMA, USEKARMA.DEV and KARMA?** Expand exact, dominant-term, phonetic CARMA/KHARMA, punctuation, compounds, logos/designs and translations across all relevant classes, state registers and common-law sources. What material candidates did this bounded search miss? Evidence E-Q01–08; risks R-01/R-09/R-13. Deliver dated queries/results and a reasoned assessment, not a zero-result certification.
2. **Who operates usekarma.com, and what prior rights, if any, exist?** The AI lead-scoring page, 2019 domain registration, 2020 footer and `legion.is` invite-domain clue do not prove a legal owner, first use or continuity. Establish legal identity/succession, U.S. and geographic use, current/abandoned operations and goods/channels. Distinguish Karma Shopping/Shoptagr from the lead-scoring business unless evidence connects them. Evidence E-C01–06/E-W02; R-02/R-06. No business contact is authorized by this handoff.
3. **How do AI KARMA 50119502, SAASKARMA 99905112 and KARMAPOINT 79295201 affect the actual/prospective engineering scope?** Compare AI software testing, hosting/containerized application development, database software and IT services with UseKarma's current tools and plausible hosted offerings. Evaluate wording, channels, whole-mark similarity, priority/basis and pending proceedings, not just class labels. Evidence E-F02/F10/F17/E-T01/T02; R-03/R-04.
4. **Does UseKarma have qualifying trademark use, and what evidence supports its date, geography and owner?** The founder reports infrastructure work since May 2025 and public agent-first materials in October 2026, but no first-use-in-commerce date. Which open-source downloads/releases/branding displays qualify, if any? Does activity identify USEKARMA or only an individual project? Evidence E-P01/P02/E-W01; R-05/R-12. Ask for release archives, dated pages, distribution evidence and author/title records; do not backdate use from Git history or domain registration.
5. **What applicant and filing basis would be proper if humans later decide to pursue protection?** Identify actual brand goodwill/title, intended-use holder and entity timing; assess any employment/contributor claims and intent-to-use assignment restrictions. A future LLC cannot be presumed to own current work or domains. Evidence E-P01 and [IP inventory](IP_OWNERSHIP.md)/[domain assertion](DOMAIN_INVENTORY.md); R-12. Deliver a proposed legally reviewed ownership/timing sequence, not executed instruments.
