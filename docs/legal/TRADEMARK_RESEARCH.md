## Goods, services, ownership, and costs

All guidance below researched 2026-10-13. These are classification hypotheses for review, not final application wording.

| Intended activity | Candidate treatment | Evidence / limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downloadable engineering/AI/infrastructure software, including open-source releases | Class 9; identify actual function | [USPTO goods/services](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/goods-and-services), [specimens](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/laws/specimen-refusal-and-how-overcome-refusal). GitHub source publication alone does not settle trademark use or acceptable specimen. |
| Hosted AI/SaaS, software design, engineering services, infrastructure-governance software | Class 42 may fit actual technical service | [ID Manual](https://idm-tmng.uspto.gov/id-master-list-public.html). Distinguish downloadable products from hosted services and from general business advice. |
| Business governance consulting, training, finance, or care offerings | Investigate 35/41/36/44 only if actually planned | Do not infer these services from repository names or experimental domain prototypes. |

[USPTO confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) assesses similarity in sound, appearance, meaning, and commercial impression together with related offerings/channels. [Federal search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching) warns that class narrowing can miss related goods. Different classes and the addition of USE or `.dev` cannot be presumed to avoid a conflict.

[Current base application cost](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/how-much-does-it-cost): **$350 per class** for Sections 1/44, before applicable surcharges and counsel. Hypothetical classes 9+42 yield $700 base; this is not a proposed filing. [Additional-fee guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/additional-fees-trademark-applications) describes incomplete-information and free-form-identification surcharges; consult the [live schedule](https://www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/fees-and-payment/uspto-fee-schedule) for all fees.

Actual commercial use, first-use evidence, or bona fide intent-to-use must be assessed before choosing a filing basis. [TMEP assignments guidance](https://tmep.uspto.gov/RDMS/TMEP/print?href=TMEP-500d1e1.html&version=current) restricts pre-use assignment of intent-to-use applications to qualifying business successors; an LLC that does not exist cannot simply be named as owner. Counsel must resolve applicant identity and succession before filing. [USPTO maintenance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/maintain/keeping-your-registration-alive) requires use/maintenance filings between years 5–6 and renewal/use filings between years 9–10 and every ten years afterward, subject to the applicable registration route and grace periods. Federal registration is not worldwide protection or protection for every activity.

## UseKarma versus usekarma.dev

**Proposed preference, not a legal decision:** use **UseKarma** as the organization/umbrella identity and **usekarma.dev** as the developer/documentation address, only if counsel clears the naming strategy. This accommodates AI alignment research, engineering, services, and multiple projects without making an address the organizational identity. A different legal entity name could be considered independently.

Using **usekarma.dev** as the full public brand gives a concrete developer signal and direct destination, but ties identity to a particular address and may feel narrower for research or non-developer stakeholders. It is not a clearance shortcut. Counsel should assess USEKARMA and USEKARMA.DEV separately, including whether the suffix adds meaningful distinctiveness, likely scope, specimens, and exact brand usage. If conflicts are material, investigate a more distinctive replacement before investing in either version. No renaming is implemented.
